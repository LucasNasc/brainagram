# Code of Conduct

**English** · [Português (Brasil)](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.pt-BR.md)

## Our pledge

Brainagram is made by people who enjoy drawing how software works, and we want
everyone who shows up to feel at home here, whether it's your first issue or
your hundredth pull request.

Whoever you are and wherever you come from, there's a place for you here. We
promise to be kind, patient and curious with each other. Everyone was a
beginner once, every question is a fair question, and there's always more than
one good way to draw a diagram. 💜

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps the community:

- Being kind and empathetic to other people
- Respecting opinions, viewpoints and experiences that differ from your own
- Giving constructive feedback, and accepting it gracefully
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologizing to the people affected,
  and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community as a whole, not only for
  ourselves

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing other people's private information, such as a physical or email
  address, without their explicit permission
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

The project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to
any behavior they find inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits,
issues and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct. When
they do, they will explain the reason when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all of the project's spaces: the repository,
issues, pull requests, discussions and any chat channels. It also applies when
someone officially represents the project in public spaces, for example by
posting from an official account or acting as a representative at an event.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it
**privately** to the project maintainers:

- by email, using the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile ([@LucasNasc](https://github.com/LucasNasc)), or
- through GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
  feature, available on every comment, issue and pull request.

Every report will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of the person who reported the
incident.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will use these guidelines to decide on the consequences of a
violation:

1. **Correction.** *For* inappropriate language or other behavior that is
   unprofessional or unwelcome. *Consequence:* a private written warning that
   explains why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be
   requested.
2. **Warning.** *For* a single incident or a series of actions that violate the
   code. *Consequence:* a warning with consequences for continued behavior.
   For a set period, no interaction with the people involved, including
   unsolicited interaction with the maintainers. Violating these terms may
   lead to a temporary or permanent ban.
3. **Temporary ban.** *For* a serious violation, including sustained
   inappropriate behavior. *Consequence:* a temporary ban from any interaction
   or public communication with the community for a set period. Violating
   these terms may lead to a permanent ban.
4. **Permanent ban.** *For* a pattern of violations, harassment of an
   individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of groups of people.
   *Consequence:* a permanent ban from any public interaction with the
   community.
